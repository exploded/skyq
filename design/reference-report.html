Transparency by filter — and the errors it caused

Night of 2–3 September 2026. Splitting H, O and S was the right call: raw star counts are useless across filters and targets. O III found 716 stars on IC 4628 but only 228 on NGC 2070 — a 3× difference that has nothing to do with the sky. Every frame below is therefore expressed as a percentage of its own target × filter clear‑sky median, so one line means one thing: how much of a normal night got through.

Star counts come from Hocus Focus detection on each saved 300 s sub, tagged with the filter in place at exposure start and the target then being imaged. Autofocus and short centering frames are excluded. All‑sky video timing was calibrated against eleven burned‑in timestamps and interpolated piecewise, because the capture cadence is not constant.
